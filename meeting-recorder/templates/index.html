How to capture Teams or Google Meet
  1. Join the meeting in Chrome or Edge in another tab (Meet, or Teams on the web).
  2. Press Share meeting tab and start, pick the meeting tab, and keep Also share tab audio switched on.
  3. Leave this tab open. Audio is saved to the server every 15 seconds, so a dropped connection loses very little.
  4. Using the Teams desktop app? Choose Entire screen and tick Share system audio (Windows only). On macOS, join the meeting from the browser instead.

Recording stops by itself after {{ max_minutes }} minutes. Let the other participants know the meeting is being recorded.
