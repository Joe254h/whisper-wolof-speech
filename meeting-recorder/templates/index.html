How to capture Teams or Google Meet
  1. Join the meeting in Chrome or Edge in another tab (Meet, or Teams on the web).
  2. Press the record button, pick the meeting tab, and keep Also share tab audio switched on.
  3. Leave this tab open. Audio is saved every 15 seconds, so a dropped connection loses very little.
  4. Teams desktop app: choose Entire screen and tick Share system audio (Windows only).

Recording stops by itself after {{ max_minutes }} minutes. Let the other participants know the meeting is being recorded.
